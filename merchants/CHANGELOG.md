# Changelog

## 0.1.1 (2026-11-04)

- The *Market* button keeps its place among the token tools next to other modules' buttons.

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-25)

- First release. Friendly merchants on your scenes: each placed merchant has his own stock, chosen for
  the scene's level and the same for every visitor.
- Prices per character: a global markup or discount per merchant, and sparse rules by race and
  profession - one row per exception.
- Players buy and sell on their own; the connected GM's client carries the trades out.
- Custom stock (quest items, specials), restocking by button or after a period (default: a day).
- Craft (with *Dewarim: Item templates*): a merchant's own make - random stock in a strength range
  ("+5 … +20 %") at a price change ("+10 %").
- Market window listing the merchants on a scene; dnd5e adapter (coins with change, item levels by
  rarity), a generic fallback for other systems.
